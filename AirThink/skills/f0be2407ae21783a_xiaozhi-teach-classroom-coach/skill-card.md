## Description:

Helps Chinese-language K–12 teachers turn lesson goals into classroom questions, group activities, participation strategies, and post-class observations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to plan questioning, timed group work, and responses to classroom silence, then draft aggregate observations for review after class. It advises teachers rather than teaching or assessing students on their behalf.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Chinese-language K–12 settings; localize curriculum and emergency-support channels before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classroom observations could retain sensitive student information or be shared unintentionally.

Mitigation: Review logs before saving; keep observations aggregate and use pause, delete, and sharing controls when records should not be retained or shared.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions or emergency-support guidance may not fit other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, language, and support channels before use outside Chinese-language K–12 settings.
