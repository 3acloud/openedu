## Description:

帮助初高中学生定位历史事件的时间与地点，构建时间轴和中外对照表，并练习纪年换算及历史地图识读。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

初高中学生及辅导他们的老师可用它梳理历史事件的先后、因果和同期中外联系，练习纪年换算与教材历史地图识读。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

中国大陆；其他地区须先本地化课程、语言、未成年人同意规则及危机求助渠道。

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis contacts and school curricula are tailored to Chinese K12 learners and may be inappropriate elsewhere.

Mitigation: Localize language, curriculum, minor-consent rules, and crisis-support contacts before use outside the intended setting.

Risk: Optional learning-progress records and reminders involve minors' information.

Mitigation: Review profile and reminder controls with students or guardians; enable storage and reminders only with the required consent.
