## Description:

Coaches students through paragraph- and essay-level English writing feedback on grammar, vocabulary, and structure, using questions to help them revise their own work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary, middle, and high school use this coach to improve English compositions through targeted feedback, guided revisions, writing exercises, and optional progress tracking. Primary-school students should use it with a parent or teacher present.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; emergency contacts, school-grade assumptions, and consent practices require localization outside Mainland China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning profiles and parent summaries may expose a minor's writing history without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Review and configure profile, cross-skill sharing, reminder, and parent-summary consent settings before enabling those features.

Risk: Emergency-help guidance or grade-level and minor-consent assumptions may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize emergency channels, grade expectations, and consent rules before student use in another region.
