## Description:

引导小学高段至高中学生围绕一个真实主题开展一周的跨学科探索，梳理知识联结并整理项目记录。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in upper primary through high school, with educators or guardians where appropriate, use this skill to investigate a real-world theme from multiple subjects and record their own discoveries and connections. It supports guided exploration rather than writing a research report for the student.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; other regions require localization of curriculum, emergency resources, and consent handling for minors.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students' project records may contain sensitive information about minors.

Mitigation: Enable profile memory only with configured consent rules and use the skill's confirmation-gated record updates and data controls.

Risk: Curriculum assumptions and crisis contacts may be inappropriate outside Mainland China.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, emergency resources, and minor-data consent handling before deployment in other regions.
