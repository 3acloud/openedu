## Description:

Analyzes recurring math mistakes for Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students, identifying underlying error patterns and targeted next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students and educators use this skill to identify root causes of repeated math errors, review weakness reports, and plan focused practice. It covers middle-school math and selected first-year high-school transition topics; recording and sharing learning profiles require consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term profiles and cross-skill handoffs may expose a minor's learning history without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Confirm profile, cross-skill sharing, and parent-sharing consent settings before storing or sharing records.

Risk: Crisis referrals intended for mainland China may be inappropriate in other locations.

Mitigation: Localize crisis resources and confirm the student's location before relying on emergency contacts.
