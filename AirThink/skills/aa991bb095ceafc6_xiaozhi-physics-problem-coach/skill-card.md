## Description:

Guides middle-school and first-year high-school students through individual physics problems using diagrams, modeling, calculations, and answer checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in mainland China's middle-school and first-year high-school physics courses use this skill for step-by-step help on a specific problem. Optional profile lookup, wrong-answer handoff, and reminders require separate consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent handling elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional learner-profile access, wrong-answer handoff, or reminders may affect student privacy without effective consent controls.

Mitigation: Keep these features off by default and confirm the platform enforces separate student and, where required, guardian consent gates before enabling them.

Risk: Mainland China curriculum and emergency guidance may be unsuitable in other regions.

Mitigation: Localize curriculum, emergency contacts, and minor-consent handling before deployment outside mainland China.
