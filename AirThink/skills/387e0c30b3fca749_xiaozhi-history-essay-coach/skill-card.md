## Description:

引导初高中学生从历史材料中提炼自己的观点、选取史实并检查论证，不替学生拟观点或代写答案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

面向初高中历史学习者，帮助他们独立完成开放性论述题和历史小论文，并根据史实、论证逻辑与课程标准自查。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China; adapt curriculum, safeguarding resources, and student-data consent requirements before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student learning profiles, cross-skill sharing, or reminders could expose or retain sensitive learning information without informed consent.

Mitigation: Confirm that the student or guardian understands what will be saved and shared, obtain the required consent before enabling these optional features, and explain how to pause or delete them.
