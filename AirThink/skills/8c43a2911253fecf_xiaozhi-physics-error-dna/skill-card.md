## Description:

Analyzes physics mistakes for Chinese-speaking middle-school and first-year high-school students, identifying recurring causes across diagrams, concepts, formulas, processes, and mathematical tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to diagnose patterns in physics errors and receive targeted weak-point reports and practice suggestions. With consent, it maintains a learning record that can inform later reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language educational content)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent records of a student's physics weaknesses may be retained or shared with companion skills or parents beyond the student's expectations.

Mitigation: Confirm student and guardian expectations before use; honor consent gates and the pause, deletion, export, and parent-sharing controls.

Risk: A weak-point diagnosis or trend based on too few observations may mislead the student.

Mitigation: Label sparse evidence as insufficient and avoid historical counts when cross-session records or statistics are unavailable.
