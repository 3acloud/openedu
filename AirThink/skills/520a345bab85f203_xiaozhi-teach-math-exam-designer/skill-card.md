## Description:

Helps Chinese K–12 math teachers design diagnostic assessments with test blueprints, item selection, item-level statistics, and teacher-approved record updates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Math teachers use this skill to plan assessments with two-way specification tables, select or draft candidate questions, and analyze item-level results. Teachers verify generated questions and approve any record updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential upcoming exam materials could be exposed if entered into the skill.

Mitigation: Do not provide confidential upcoming exam questions, answers, or scoring keys.

Risk: Unverified generated questions or automatic record updates could affect assessments.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check each candidate question and confirm every record update before use.

Risk: Use outside its Chinese K–12 setting may not match local curriculum, privacy requirements, or emergency contacts.

Mitigation: Localize the curriculum, language, privacy rules, and emergency contacts before deploying elsewhere.
