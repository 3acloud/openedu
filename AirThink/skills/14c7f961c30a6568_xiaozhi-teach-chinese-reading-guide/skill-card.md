## Description:

帮助语文教师设计现代文阅读教学，提供文本解读、阅读策略、分层练习和群文阅读的教学框架。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

语文教师使用此技能准备小学高段和初中的现代文阅读课：梳理文本层次、选择阅读策略、设计课堂与课后任务，并在获授权时整理班级阅读记录。教学内容须经教师审定后使用。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student reading records may contain sensitive information or reach parents or other skills without permission.

Mitigation: Follow school consent, retention and approved-storage rules; check the relevant consent flags before record writeback or parent sharing, and honor pause, delete and export controls.

Risk: AI-generated reading questions or teaching plans may be inaccurate or unsuitable for students.

Mitigation: Have the teacher check generated questions against the source text and review all student-facing materials before classroom use.

Risk: Reproducing textbook passages or ebooks without permission may infringe copyright.

Mitigation: Use short teaching outlines and source indexes rather than copying full textbooks or unauthorized ebooks.
