## Description:

通过每周的兴趣探索，帮助学生观察遇到困难时的反应，区分浅层喜好与可能持续的兴趣。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students in middle elementary school through high school use guided weekly activities to explore their interests and reflect on challenges. Longer-term records require informed student consent and, where required, guardian consent; the skill does not provide academic tutoring or career and admissions recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global (Chinese-language guidance; adapt minor-data consent and crisis contacts to local requirements)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long-term interest records about minors could be collected or shared without informed consent.

Mitigation: Explain retention, access, sharing, and deletion in Chinese before tracking; obtain student and required guardian consent, confirm each write, and honor view, pause, sharing, export, and deletion controls.

Risk: Consent and emergency-contact assumptions may not apply outside China mainland.

Mitigation: Check applicable minor-data rules and guardian requirements and confirm the student's location before giving local emergency contacts.

Risk: A student may disclose self-harm, abuse, or another safety crisis during exploration.

Mitigation: Stop the exploration workflow and follow the crisis referral guidance, encouraging contact with a trusted adult and locally appropriate help.
